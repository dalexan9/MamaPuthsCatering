About Mama Puth Catering Events

Mama Puth Catering Events is dedicated to preserving generational recipes and offering refined event decoration for gatherings of any size—from intimate family occasions to large-scale celebrations. Our services extend to family events, church functions, non-profit initiatives, and business gatherings.

We strive to make every event distinctive by blending exceptional cuisine, elegant presentation, and meticulous attention to detail. Years of experience have cultivated a passion for designing memorable meals and sophisticated event settings.

Through both culinary artistry and tasteful décor, we transform ordinary occasions into extraordinary experiences.

For every booking or order with Mama Puth Catering Events, 10% of proceeds will go to support local communities and charities such as St. Jude’s Children Hospital, Wheels for the World, and overseas charitable organizations. Thank you for selecting Mama Puth Catering Events. Blessings!
